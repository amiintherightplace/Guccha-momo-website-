Momo, served by the bunch.

Steamed momo in warm golden jhol with red chilli on top, and skewers on the side. Pull up a wooden table and eat it while it's hot.

Explore Menu Reserve a Table

Guccha means a bunch, so that's how the momo come.

The idea is simple: a foil bowl of steamed momo, warm jhol poured over, a spoon of red chilli on top. Then something off a stick on the side.

There is nothing fancy about the setting, and that's the point. Wooden tables, foil bowls, plastic spoons, and food meant to be eaten while it's hot.

Start with jhol momo
Steamed momo in a warm, golden jhol, finished with chilli.
Add something off the stick
Skewers in a golden masala or a glossy orange glaze.
Share the table
Order a few things and pass them around.
